<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><b>Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.</b></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; background-color: white">On August 7, 2023, Lucid LLC, a wholly
owned subsidiary of Lucid Group,&#160;Inc. (collectively, the &#8220;<b><i>Company</i></b>&#8221;), entered into an Electric
Vehicles Purchase Agreement (the &#8220;<b><i>Agreement</i></b>&#8221;) with the Government of Saudi Arabia as represented by the
Ministry of Finance (the &#8220;<b><i>Government</i></b>&#8221;). The Agreement supersedes the Letter of Undertaking, which was
entered into on April&#160;20, 2022 (the &#8220;<b><i>Letter of Undertaking</i></b>&#8221;), by and between Lucid LLC and the
Government, pursuant to which the Government and its entities and corporate subsidiaries undertook to purchase up to 100,000
vehicles from the Company over a ten-year period, with an initial commitment to purchase 50,000 vehicles and an option to purchase
up to an additional 50,000 vehicles during such ten-year period. The Letter of Undertaking was previously filed as an exhibit to the
Company&#8217;s Quarterly Report on Form&#160;10-Q for the quarter ended March&#160;31, 2022.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; background-color: white"><span style="font-family: Times New Roman, Times, Serif; font-size: 10pt">Under
the terms of the Agreement, the Government and other beneficiaries specified in the Agreement (collectively, the
&#8220;<b><i>Purchaser</i></b>&#8221;) will purchase, whether through outright purchase or otherwise, up to 100,000 vehicles from
the Company during the Term (as defined below) pursuant to purchase orders, with a minimum purchase quantity of 50,000 vehicles and
an option to purchase up to an additional 50,000 vehicles pursuant to a schedule of annual vehicle purchase quantities set forth therein. The Purchaser may reduce the minimum purchase quantity by the number of
