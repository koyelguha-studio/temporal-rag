<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: right"><B>Exhibit 99.1</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: center"><B>Lucid Announces Q2 Production&nbsp;&amp; Deliveries,
Sets Date for Second Quarter 2024 Results</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">NEWARK, Calif., July&nbsp;8, 2024 /PRNewswire/ -- Lucid Group,&nbsp;Inc.
(NASDAQ: LCID), maker of the world's most advanced electric vehicles, today announced production and delivery totals for the quarter ended
June&nbsp;30, 2024. Lucid produced 2,110 vehicles and delivered 2,394 vehicles during this period.<SUP>1</SUP></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">Lucid will host a conference call to discuss its second quarter 2024
financial results on Monday, August&nbsp;5, 2024, at 2:30 pm PT / 5:30 pm ET. Prior to the conference call, the company will issue an
earnings press release with a link to the live webcast on its investor relations website,&nbsp;<U>https://ir.lucidmotors.com</U>.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">To enhance engagement with the company's shareholder base and facilitate
connections with its investors, Lucid is partnering with Say Technologies to allow retail and institutional shareholders to submit and
upvote questions, a selection of which will be answered by Lucid management during the earnings call.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">Starting on July&nbsp;22, 2024, at 2:30 pm PT / 5:30 pm ET, shareholders
can submit questions by visiting: <U>https://app.saytechnologies.com/lucid-group-2024-q2</U>. This Q&amp;A platform will remain open
until 2:30 pm PT / 5:30 pm ET on August&nbsp;2, 2024. Shareholders can email <U>support@saytechnologies.com</U> for any support inquiries.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>Date:</B> Monday, August&nbsp;5, 2024</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>Time:</B> 2:30 pm PT / 5:30 pm ET</P>
