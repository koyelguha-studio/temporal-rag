<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; background-color: white">Lucid Group,&#160;Inc.
(the &#8220;<b><i>Company</i></b>&#8221;) unveiled the Lucid Gravity, a luxury sport utility vehicle, to the public at the Los Angeles
Auto Show in November&#160;2023, which marked a major technical, commercial, and design milestone achievement for the Company.  On February&#160;9, 2024, the Compensation and Human Capital Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company
(the &#8220;<b><i>Board</i></b>&#8221;) recommended and the Board approved payment of a cash bonus to the Company&#8217;s Chief Executive
Officer and Chief Technology Officer, Peter Rawlinson, in the amount of $6,000,000, in recognition of his significant contributions to
this milestone achievement.</p>
