<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0 0pt 0.25in; text-indent: -0.25in"><B>Lucid Announces Q3 Production&nbsp;&amp;

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">NEWARK, Calif., October&nbsp;17, 2023 /PRNewswire/ -- Lucid Group,&nbsp;Inc.
(NASDAQ: LCID), setting new standards for luxury electric experience with the Lucid Air, winner of the 2023 World Luxury Car Award, today
announced production and delivery totals for the quarter ended September&nbsp;30, 2023. Lucid produced 1,550 vehicles during Q3, plus
over 700 additional vehicles in transit to Saudi Arabia for final assembly. The company delivered 1,457 vehicles during the same period.*</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">Lucid will host a conference call to discuss its third quarter 2023

an earnings press release with a link to the live webcast on its investor relations website,&nbsp;<U>https://ir.lucidmotors.com</U>.</P>
