<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify"><i>2023 Equity Long-Term Incentive Program</i></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">On September 10, 2023, the
Board of Directors (the &#8220;<b>Board</b>&#8221;) of Lucid Group, Inc. (the &#8220;<b>Company</b>&#8221;), upon the recommendation of
the Compensation Committee of the Board (the &#8220;<b>Compensation Committee</b>&#8221;), approved the below annual equity awards for
the below named executive officers of the Company (the &#8220;<b>NEOs</b>&#8221;) pursuant to a new 2023 equity award framework, comprised
of restricted stock units (&#8220;<b>RSUs</b>&#8221;), performance stock units (&#8220;<b>PSUs</b>&#8221;) (based on the target number

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in"><span style="text-decoration: underline">PSUs</span></p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">The number of PSUs earned
pursuant to the PSU awards will be between 0% and 150% of an NEO&#8217;s target number of PSUs and will be determined based on the level
of achievement of revenue, gross margin, and free cash flow performance goals (the &#8220;<b>Performance Goals</b>&#8221;), in each case,
as measured over the period from January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2023 (the &#8220;<b>Performance Period</b>&#8221;) and as adjusted
by an individual performance multiplier. Any earned PSUs will vest (i) 1/3 on the later of March 5, 2024 and the date that the level of
achievement with respect to the Performance Goals is certified and (ii) 2/3 in equal quarterly installments thereafter, subject to the
NEO&#8217;s continued employment through the applicable vesting dates.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&#160;</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">In the event of a Change
in Control (as defined in the Company&#8217;s Second Amended and Restated 2021 Stock Incentive Plan (the &#8220;<b>Plan</b>&#8221;))
or any other significant corporate transaction, in each case, during the Performance Period, performance with respect to the
Performance Goals will be deemed satisfied at the greater of actual and target performance. Subject to the terms of the
Company&#8217;s Executive Severance Benefit Plan, the PSU awards, to the extent earned, will otherwise remain outstanding following
such a transaction and will vest subject to the NEO&#8217;s continued employment through the applicable vesting dates.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">&#160;</p>

is subject to, and qualified in its entirety by, the Plan and the PSU Agreement, the terms of which are incorporated herein by reference.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0; text-align: justify; text-indent: 0.5in">The Premium-Priced Options
will vest over four years, with 5/48 vesting on the first monthly anniversary of the grant date, and the remainder vesting in equal monthly
installments of 1/48, subject to the NEO&#8217;s continued employment through the applicable vesting dates. The Premium-Priced Options
were granted under the Plan using an option award agreement substantially consistent with the form of option award agreement previously
filed by the Company. The exercise price for the Premium-Priced Options was determined based on the greater of (i) 125% of the 30-day
volume-weighted average closing price (determined as of the grant date) of the Company&#8217;s common stock and (ii) the closing price
of the Company&#8217;s common stock on the grant date.</p>
