completion of the public offering and customary closing conditions. As a result of these purchases, Ayar expects to maintain its approximate
58.8% ownership of Lucid&rsquo;s outstanding common stock. In addition, Ayar has indicated that they intend to purchase from us, in the
event that the underwriter exercises its option, additional shares of our common stock to maintain its ownership of Lucid&rsquo;s outstanding
common stock, and an additional closing for such purchase would be held 10 days after the underwriter&rsquo;s exercise of its option.</p>

<p style="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">Lucid intends to use the net proceeds from the public offering, as
