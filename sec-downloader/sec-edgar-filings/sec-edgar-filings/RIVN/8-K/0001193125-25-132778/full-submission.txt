<td style="vertical-align:top;text-align:left"> <p style=" margin-top:0pt ; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold;text-align:left">Regulation FD Disclosure. </p></td></tr></table> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">On June&#160;2, 2025, Rivian Automotive, Inc. (&#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Rivian</span>&#8221;) announced that Rivian Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (&#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Holdings</span>&#8221;), Rivian, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (&#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Rivian</span><span style="font-weight:bold"> LLC</span>&#8221;) and Rivian Automotive, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (&#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Rivian</span><span style="font-weight:bold"> Automotive</span>&#8221; and, together with Holdings and Rivian LLC, the &#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold"><span style="white-space:nowrap">Co-Issuers</span></span><span style="font-weight:bold">&#8221;</span> and each a wholly-owned subsidiary of Rivian) intend to offer, subject to market and other customary conditions, $1,250&#160;million in aggregate principal amount of senior secured green notes due 2031 (the &#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Notes</span>&#8221;) in a private offering (the &#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Notes Offering</span>&#8221;) to persons reasonably believed to be qualified institutional buyers pursuant to Rule 144A under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the &#8220;<span style="font-weight:bold">Securities Act</span>&#8221;), and to <span style="white-space:nowrap">non-U.S.</span> persons pursuant to Regulation S under the Securities Act.
