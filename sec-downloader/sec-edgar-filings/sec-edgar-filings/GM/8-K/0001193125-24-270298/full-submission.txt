<td align="left" style="vertical-align:top"> <p style=" margin-top:0pt ; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman;font-weight:bold;text-align:left">Material Impairments </p></td></tr></table> <p style="margin-top:6pt; margin-bottom:0pt; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman">General Motors Company (the &#8220;Company&#8221;, &#8220;we&#8221; or &#8220;our&#8221;) owns an equity interest in SAIC General Motors Corporation Limited (&#8220;SGM&#8221;), a <span style="white-space:nowrap">50-50</span> joint venture with SAIC Motor Corp., Ltd. (&#8220;SAIC&#8221;), and an equity interest in SAIC-GMAC Automotive Finance Company Limited (&#8220;SAIC-GMAC&#8221;). SGM conducts automotive operations in China through various other joint ventures with GM (together with SGM and SAIC-GMAC, the &#8220;China JVs&#8221;). On December&#160;2, 2024, the Audit Committee of the Board of Directors of the Company concluded a material impairment of the Company&#8217;s interest in SGM was required based on a determination that a material loss in value of our investments in certain of the China JVs is other than temporary in light of the finalization of a new business forecast and certain restructuring actions that SGM is finalizing that are expected to be taken to address market challenges and competitive conditions. The Company is in the process of assessing the impact of SGM&#8217;s planned restructuring actions and recent efforts to stabilize market share and focus on profitability, and expects to (i)&#160;record an other than temporary impairment of our equity interest in the China JVs in the range of $2.6&#8211;2.9&#160;billion in the three months ending December&#160;31, 2024, and (ii)&#160;recognize additional equity losses of approximately $2.7&#160;billion resulting from the implementation of SGM&#8217;s restructuring plan, which include impairment charges to be recognized by the China JVs related to plant closures and portfolio optimization, the majority of which we expect to record in the three months ending December&#160;31, 2024.
